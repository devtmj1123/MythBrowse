      Welcome to MythBrowse

      A browser with Myth built in. Ask in the address bar or on a new tab: Myth reads pages for you and, with your OK, acts on them.

      • BrowsePages float on a living sky. Site fixes step in when a page breaks.
      • Your modelMyth runs on an API key you own or on Ollama on this PC.
      • You decideMyth asks before it sends, signs in, buys or deletes anything.

      Set up Myth

      Choose where Myth's model runs. You can change it any time from the Myth pill.

      Bring your things

      Import bookmarks, history and passwords from Chrome or Edge, or a password file. Optional, and it can wait.
