    Site compatibility
    Site fixesMythBrowse runs on Servo. When a page breaks, built-in fixes (polyfills, per-site rules) step in automatically.
    Rule updates Automatically update site-fix rules: signed, declarative fixes that help sites work in this engine. Nothing about you is sent.
    Per siteOpen a page, then the compat icon by the bookmark star: re-check, reader view, “Ask Myth to fix”, and turn a site’s fixes off or on.

    Sync

    Sign in to sync bookmarks, history and Myth chats across your computers. Everything is encrypted on this device first; the server only stores ciphertext.

    Choose a sync passphrase. It is separate from your account password and never leaves this computer; your other computers need it to read your data.

    This account already syncs. Enter its sync passphrase.

    Enter your recovery code and choose a new sync passphrase.

    Save your recovery code. It is the only way back in if you forget the sync passphrase. It is shown once.
